“Step Beyond the Ordinary Client” — a visual UI concept for the Void Launcher. Every screen is a static mockup: buttons and toggles are styled illustrations, not wired logic.

Notes & honesty: The character-render area in View 01 is an intentional drop-in slot — supply your own licensed Cid Kagenou / “The Eminence in Shadow” artwork (the anime is © Daisuke Aizawa / KADOKAWA); this mockup ships no copyrighted art. Icons are hand-built inline SVG in the dark-fantasy style (the requested HyperFrames / mustbesimo generators weren't available in this session). Not affiliated with Mojang, Microsoft, Discord, Hypixel, CurseForge or Modrinth — names shown are integration references only.
